> If nobody noticed, would you still do it?
{: .prompt-tip }

## My Nine Reflections from the Bhagavad Gita

### 1. Action

> *Every action you take will carry some smoke and someone will misunderstand it.*

> **Act consciously, remaining present as the Witness.**
>
> No action is free from imperfection, just as no fire is free from smoke. If we wait for an action that nobody can criticise, we may never act at all. Examine the [fire behind the action](/posts/finding-your-true-purpose-in-the-heat-of-action/), not the smoke around it.
{: .prompt-tip }

### 2. Effort

> *The fruits of your work are not yours to command.*

> **Work with your whole heart.**
>
> You have a right to your action, but not to its results. Give your effort fully, then let it go. This is [Authentic Action](/posts/the-power-of-stepping-back-into-authentic-action/).
{: .prompt-tip }

### 3. Speech

> *Speaking the truth with kindness takes more effort than staying silent or being blunt.*

> **Speak the truth gently.**
>
> Speech should be non-agitating, truthful, and beneficial. It is easy to tell it like it is but it is harder to make sure [the truth can actually be received](/posts/the-transformative-power-of-mindful-speech/).
{: .prompt-tip }

### 4. Boundaries

> *Saying "no" may cost you approval and some will call it unkind.*

> **Hold the boundary with an open heart.**
>
> A boundary set from [Samatvam](/posts/how-setting-boundaries-is-an-act-of-love/), or equanimity, is a filter and not a wall. You can refuse a behaviour while [still honouring the person](/posts/protecting-your-peace-without-closing-your-heart/).
{: .prompt-tip }

### 5. Path

> *Your path might look less impressive than someone else's.*

> **Walk your own path.**
>
> It is better to follow one's own Dharma imperfectly than another's perfectly. A puzzle piece that reshapes itself to look more "successful" [no longer fits](/posts/aligning-your-purpose-with-the-rhythm-of-life/).
{: .prompt-tip }

### 6. Change

> *Everything you love and build will change. Ice is meant to melt.*

> **Love and build without clinging.**
>
> Pleasure and pain come and go like winter and summer. Enjoy the [museum of ice](/posts/finding-grace-in-an-ever-changing-world/) while accepting that it will melt and seek [stability in the Witness](/posts/finding-stability-in-the-changing-world/) rather than in the scenery.
{: .prompt-tip }

### 7. Service

> *The people you help may never thank you and your ego may want them to.*

> **Serve as an offering.**
>
> Offer your actions without possessiveness. The receiver is not beneath you, they are [giving you a chance to practise humility](/posts/the-beautiful-dance-of-generosity/).
{: .prompt-tip }

### 8. Solitude

> *Silence will feel empty and your mind will grow restless when you are alone.*

> **Sit with yourself.**
>
> The mind can be your friend or your enemy. A friendly mind is built through [patient attention](/posts/how-a-quiet-friday-night-turns-the-mind-into-a-friend/), not constant distraction.
{: .prompt-tip }

### 9. The Unseen

> *The good you do today may go unseen and tomorrow it may be forgotten.*

> **Do it for the whole, not for the audience.**
>
> The Gita calls us to Lokasamgraha, acting for the welfare of the world. When the work is [offered to something beyond you](/posts/staying-true-to-yourself-when-everyone-is-watching/), being forgotten no longer feels like being erased.
{: .prompt-tip }
